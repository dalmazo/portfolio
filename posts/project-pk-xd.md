## Technical Artist (UI)

**Afterverse**

I was responsible for LiveOps integrations and UI integration for several
features. Most of my work was on LiveOps events: exporting assets from Figma,
creating/updating atlases, checking the compression method, and assigning
Addressables.

I also worked on a communication feature built entirely around emotes/emojis.
This mattered a lot because our audience was mostly kids who were still
learning to read — emojis were their language. The emotes were implemented
using TextMeshPro.
